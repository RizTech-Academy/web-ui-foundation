What we teach

Three kinds of class, each running several times a week. If you have never done yoga before, start with hatha — it is slower, and the teacher will talk you through every posture.

  • Hatha

    Slow, held postures with time to breathe and adjust. The place to begin.

    60 minutes · all levels

  • Vinyasa

    Postures linked into a continuous sequence, matched to the breath.

    60 minutes · some experience helps

  • Pranayama

    Seated breathing practice. No flexibility required, and no mat needed.

    40 minutes · all levels
